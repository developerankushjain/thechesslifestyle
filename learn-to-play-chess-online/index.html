The Basics — Answered

How Do You Actually
Learn Chess Online?

Honest answers from coaches who've taught thousands of beginners. No jargon.

Do I need any prior knowledge?

None at all. Our 0-Level programme starts from absolute zero — we introduce each piece using stories and visual analogies. By the end of lesson one, you'll know how every piece on the board moves.

How long does it take to learn chess?

You'll understand all the basic rules in 2–3 sessions (about 2 weeks). Playing confidently takes 4–8 weeks. Reaching Chess.com 1000 typically takes 3–6 months with weekly coaching. There's no rush — we go at your pace.

Should I learn openings or tactics first?

Tactics first — always. Our coaches build your tactical eye (forks, pins, skewers) before teaching openings. Why? Because you need to understand why a move is good before memorising sequences. Openings come naturally after 2–3 months.

How do online classes actually work?

Live on Zoom with a shared board on Lichess — no download required. Your coach moves pieces on a shared screen, explains concepts in plain English, and gives you puzzles to solve in real time. After each class, a detailed report lands in your parent dashboard.

Our Curriculum

A Learning Path for
Every Level

Whether you've never touched a chessboard or you're aiming for a FIDE rating — we have a structured programme built just for you.

0

0-Level — Absolute Beginner (I don't know how the pieces move)

We start from the very beginning. You'll learn how each piece moves, basic board orientation, the goal of the game (checkmate!), and simple patterns. Taught through interactive games and visual activities — perfect for kids aged 5–8 and adults picking up chess for the first time.

Age 5+ · No rating needed · Most popular starting point
1

Foundation — I know the rules, want to win more games

Piece movement mastery, basic checkmating patterns (Scholar's Mate, back rank), first tactical themes (fork, pin), and opening principles (centre control, piece development). You'll go from "surviving games" to "understanding positions."

Chess.com 0–800 · 8–16 weeks typical
2

Beginner — I want to reach Chess.com 1000+

Intermediate tactical patterns, clean opening repertoire (Italian, London, or Caro-Kann depending on your style), pawn structure theory, and essential endgame techniques (King+Pawn vs King, Rook endings). Real rating growth begins here.

Chess.com 800–1200 · 3–6 months typical
3

Intermediate — Building a serious playing style

Long-term positional planning, opening repertoire deepening, calculation training, dynamic vs. static evaluation, and tournament psychology. This is where chess becomes truly fascinating — and competitive.

Chess.com 1200–1500 · Ongoing development
4

Advanced — Tournament & FIDE Rating Track

Deep game analysis, novelty preparation, complex endgame studies, and FIDE/USCF/UKCA tournament preparation. Work directly with coaches rated 1600+ FIDE who have competed at state and national levels.

Chess.com 1500–1800+ · FIDE preparation
