Which Option is Right for Your Child?

Answer these simple questions to identify the ideal coaching format for your child's current development stage.

Choose Group Classes (₹3,999/mo) If:

  • ✓ Your child is between 5 and 11 years old and learning chess fundamentals.
  • ✓ They get bored studying alone and enjoy solving puzzles with peers.
  • ✓ You want consistent, structured weekly training at an affordable price.
  • ✓ They want friendly in-house practice tournaments before entering official federations.
  • ✓ You value transparent 24/7 parent tracking via our in-house CRM system.

Choose Private 1-on-1 (₹8,000/mo) If:

  • ✓ Your child is preparing for official USCF, ECF, or FIDE scholastic tournaments.
  • ✓ They have hit a rating plateau (e.g. stuck at 800 or 1200) and need surgical diagnosis.
  • ✓ Your family requires 100% flexible scheduling due to unpredictable school/sports commitments.
  • ✓ They need personalized puzzle homework tailored to their exact blunder history.
  • ✓ They learn much faster with 100% uninterrupted master attention.
