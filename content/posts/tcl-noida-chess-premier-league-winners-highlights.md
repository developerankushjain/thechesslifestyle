## A Celebration of Scholastic Strategy & Sportsmanship

On **Saturday, 31st October 2026**, chess passion in Delhi-NCR reached a fever pitch as **TheChessLifestyle (TCL)** hosted the prestigious **1st TCL Noida Chess Premier League** at the expansive auditorium of **Raghav Global School, Sector 122, Noida**.

From budding 6-year-old prodigies playing their very first rated board tournament to seasoned open-category competitors calculating deep rook endgames, the arena witnessed extraordinary tactical brilliance, nail-biting time scrambles, and heartwarming sportsmanship across every single round.

### Key Tournament Statistics:
- **Total Registered Participants:** 89 players
- **Championship Categories:** 2 (Under-12 and Open)
- **Venue:** Raghav Global School, Noida Sector 122
- **Rounds:** 6 rounds of classical rapid time controls
- **Satisfaction Rating:** 96%+ top-tier 5-star & 4-star ratings in post-event community poll
