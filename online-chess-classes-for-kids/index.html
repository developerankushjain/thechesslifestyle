The Science

What Chess Does for Your Child's Brain

Chess is backed by decades of peer-reviewed research as one of the most effective cognitive development tools for children.

Laser-Sharp Focus & Concentration

Chess requires sustained attention for 30–60 minutes per game. Children who train regularly develop significantly better classroom focus and reduced distraction.

Improved Math & Academic Scores

Pattern recognition and spatial reasoning used in chess directly translate to mathematical ability. Parents consistently report measurable grade improvements.

Critical Thinking & Problem Solving

Every chess position is a unique puzzle. Children learn to evaluate multiple options, anticipate consequences, and choose the best solution under pressure.

Emotional Intelligence & Resilience

Learning to handle wins and losses gracefully builds lifelong emotional maturity. Chess teaches children that setbacks are opportunities, not failures.

Confidence & Social Skills

Competing in our in-house online tournaments gives children real experience of performing under pressure, building self-confidence that extends well beyond the chessboard.

ADHD & Focus Management

Chess is clinically recognised as one of the most effective non-pharmaceutical tools for improving impulse control and sustained attention in children with ADHD.

Common Parent Concerns

We've heard every question. Here are the honest answers parents ask most before enrolling their child.

"My child has never played chess. Is it too late to start?"
Absolutely not. Our 0-Level programme is designed for complete beginners from age 5. Many of our highest-rated students today had never touched a chess piece when they joined us.
"Will my child stay engaged in an online class?"
Yes — our sessions use interactive Lichess boards, puzzles, mini-games, and challenges that make online learning more engaging than classroom chess clubs. Most children actively request more sessions.
"How will I know if my child is actually improving?"
After every session, you receive a detailed progress note from the coach through our CRM. Monthly reports include rating progression, areas of strength, and next learning goals.
"What if my child loses interest after a few classes?"
We use a progressive "unlock" system where students earn new curriculum levels as they improve. In-house tournaments, badges, and friendly competitions keep motivation sky-high. And with a free trial, there's zero risk to try.
